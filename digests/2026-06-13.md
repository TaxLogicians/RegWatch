### 🟡 2026-10841 — relevance 2 (international)
**Type:** Proposed Rule (partial withdrawal + new applicability dates) · **IRC:** §892 · **Comment deadline:** **2026-07-31**
**Source:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/01/2026-10841/income-of-foreign-governments-and-of-international-organizations
**Plain English:** Re-proposes the applicability dates for the §892 rules on when a foreign government's debt acquisition is "commercial activity" and when it has "effective control" of an entity — i.e., when sovereign / government investors keep or lose the §892 income exemption.
**Why you care:** International — foreign-government / sovereign-investor regime. This is the Federal Register companion to the item in **IRB 2026-25** (CC-00349656-26); the FR record is what supplies the comment deadline the IRB blurb lacked.

### 🟡 2026-09989 — relevance 2 (international)
**Type:** Notice · **IRC:** §999 (international boycott) · **Comment deadline:** none
**Source:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/19/2026-09989/list-of-countries-requiring-cooperation-with-an-international-boycott
**Plain English:** Treasury's updated list of countries that require cooperation with an international boycott (the Ribicoff/§999 list).
**Why you care:** International — this list drives **Form 5713** international-boycott reporting and can cost clients foreign tax credit / deferral benefits if they operate in (or with) a listed country. Recurring, but the contents change.

### 🟡 2026-10116 — relevance 2 (international — verify scope)
**Type:** Final regulations · **IRC:** §6050K (partnership interest transfer reporting) · **Comment deadline:** n/a (final)
**Source:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/20/2026-10116/returns-relating-to-sales-or-exchanges-of-certain-partnership-interests
**Plain English:** Final regs modifying information reporting on sales/exchanges of certain partnership interests.
**Why you care:** Potentially international — partnership-interest dispositions by **foreign** persons implicate §1446(f) withholding and §864(c)(8) gain. It's a *final* reg (effective), so read it to confirm whether foreign transferors/transferees are in scope; if so, this is higher priority than its score.
