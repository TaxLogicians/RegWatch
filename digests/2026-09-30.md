## Score 2 — read this week

### 🟡 REG-117273-25 (FR doc 2026-18645) — Allocation and Apportionment of Deductions to Foreign Source §951A Category Income and Deduction Eligible Income — relevance 2 (anti_deferral / foreign_tax_credit)
**Type:** Notice of proposed rulemaking (FR 2026-09-11; IRB 2026-40) · **IRC:** §§ 250(b)(3), 904(b)(5), 864(e)(2); Reg. §1.861-8 · **Comment deadline:** 2026-11-10 (from the FR DATES section; the API `comments_close_on` field is empty)
**Source:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/11/2026-18645/allocation-and-apportionment-of-deductions-to-foreign-source-section-951a-category-income-and · https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-40_IRB
**Plain English:** Proposes how deductions are allocated and apportioned to foreign-source §951A category income when computing the FTC limitation, and when computing deduction eligible income for FDDEI. Per the IRB summary, certain deductions (interest, R&E, and non-directly-allocable deductions) would be reallocated to US-source income, reflecting OBBBA changes for tax years beginning after 2025-12-31.
**Why you care:** anti_deferral and foreign_tax_credit buckets. It changes the §904 limitation for §951A inclusions, which affects the GILTI/NCTI FTC for any US shareholder of a CFC. This is the only new substantive international NPRM that first appeared in this window.

### 🟡 REG-115646-25 (appears to be FR doc 2026-17365) — Pro Rata Share of Subpart F Income, Tested Income, or Tested Loss — relevance 2 (anti_deferral; possible intl_info_returns angle)
**Type:** Notice of proposed rulemaking (FR 2026-08-26; now in IRB 2026-39) · **IRC:** §§ 951, 951A, 958, 245A, 960 (from last run); OBBBA §70354(c)(2) transition rule · **Comment deadline:** 2026-10-26
**Source:** https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-39_IRB · https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/26/2026-17365/pro-rata-share-of-subpart-f-income-tested-income-or-tested-loss
**Plain English:** This was the top item last run. The IRB now supplies the REG number, and adds that the NPRM **"would also modify certain information reporting requirements"** and folds in the Notice 2025-75 transition rules. The IRB blurb does not say which reporting requirements; check whether Form 5471 or §6038 are affected.
**Why you care:** anti_deferral bucket. It changes §951/§951A inclusions when CFC ownership shifts mid-year. If the reporting changes land on Form 5471, this becomes a core intl_info_returns item (3), so read the reporting section of the NPRM.

### 🟡 REG-103844-26 (match not verified: likely FR doc 2026-16569) — Foreign Currency Gain or Loss of Controlled Foreign Corporations — relevance 2 (foreign_corp_partnership)
**Type:** Notice of proposed rulemaking (FR 2026-08-14; IRB 2026-37) · **IRC:** §987 · **Comment deadline:** 2026-11-12 (per the FR API for 2026-16569)
**Source:** https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-37_IRB · https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/14/2026-16569/foreign-currency-gain-or-loss-of-controlled-foreign-corporations
**Plain English:** Proposes an election under which CFCs would not compute or recognize §987 foreign currency gain or loss on qualified business units, except in connection with certain inbound transactions.
**Why you care:** foreign_corp_partnership bucket (CFC). It could simplify E&P and subpart F/tested-income computations for CFCs with foreign-currency branches, and it may affect Form 5471 schedules for those structures.

### 🟡 REG-117130-25 (match not verified: likely FR doc 2026-17019) — Exclusion of certain income from deduction eligible income / §250(b)(3)(A)(i)(VII) sales or dispositions of property — relevance 2 (anti_deferral: FDII)
**Type:** Notice of proposed rulemaking (FR 2026-08-20; IRB 2026-38) · **IRC:** §250(b)(3)(A)(i)(VII) · **Comment deadline:** 2026-10-05 (per the FR API for 2026-17019)
