**Source:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/03/2026-15614/section-898c-transition-rule-for-allocating-foreign-taxes-and-section-960d4-foreign-tax-credit
**Plain English:** Proposed regs address how a CFC allocates foreign taxes after repeal of the one-month deferral election under §898(c), and limit the foreign tax credit under §960(d)(4) on distributions of previously taxed earnings and profits (PTEP).
